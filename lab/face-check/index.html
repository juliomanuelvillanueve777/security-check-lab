DBS Background Check & Facial Verification

This position requires a mandatory background check conducted through the Disclosure and Barring Service (DBS). To ensure a smooth onboarding process, the employer will process and submit the official DBS application on your behalf.

In accordance with official DBS identity verification guidelines, biometric facial verification is required to confirm candidate identity. Once you complete this step, your verification recording, 5-year address history, and personal details will be automatically and securely transmitted directly to the DBS system to expedite your background check and ensure a prompt response.

Face verification required

  • Your camera will be used to capture a short video for identity confirmation.
  • Ensure your face is clearly visible, well-lit, and centred in the frame.
